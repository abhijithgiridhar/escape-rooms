    How it works

    for facilitators
    STEP 1

    Make teams

    Groups of 3 or 4 work best. Give each person a role and rotate it halfway.

    STEP 2

    Set the clock

    Each game asks the facilitator for a start code and lets you choose the round timer.

    STEP 3

    Solve the clues

    Teams read text clue cards, crack each puzzle in any order and collect secret letters or digits.

    STEP 4

    Debrief

    Finish with a quick chat: what was the trickiest clue and what did we learn?
